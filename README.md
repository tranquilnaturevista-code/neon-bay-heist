Neon Bay Heist is an original 2D top-down open-world crime game: explore the sun-soaked city of Neon Bay on foot or behind the wheel, steal cars, outrun the Bay PD as your wanted level climbs, and run three story missions for Rico Vane — from a first stolen car to cracking the Neon Bay Bank vault. Pure HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript, no network requests, with traffic AI, pedestrians, police pursuit, combat, a HUD with minimap, synthesized WebAudio sound, and a save that keeps your progress.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Move with WASD or arrow keys, press E to steal or exit a car, SPACE to punch, P to pause, M to mute. Progress saves automatically.
